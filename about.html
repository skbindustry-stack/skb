Our Story

Built in Coimbatore.

S.K.B. Industries has been engineering reliable water pumps since 2001 — with an in-house foundry, 120+ models, and a commitment to quality that never wavers.

India's Engineering City Builds India's Best Pumps

S.K.B. Industries is a leading manufacturer of water pumps for domestic and agricultural needs, started in the year 2001, located in the engineering capital of India — Coimbatore.

We produce more than 120 models of pumps with state-of-the-art technology. Our product competence range and business transparency place SKB Industries at the peak of values that continue to guide us into the future.

Bindhu S. Balasundaram
Founder & Managing Director, S.K.B. Industries

What started as a vision to deliver quality water pumps to Indian farmers and households has grown into a company with branches in Chennai, Kolkata, and Kerala — serving customers across the country.

Our Manufacturing

Bindhu Foundry — Where Every Pump Begins

At Bindhu Foundry, technology has always been at the forefront of our growth. Our products reflect our competence in ensuring best-in-class quality for our customers.

Over the last 15 years, our passion to upgrade to newer technologies has been a process of continued development and conditioning to best manufacturing practices. We use the DISAMATIC C3 — a state-of-the-art automated moulding machine that ensures precision-cast grey iron components for every pump.

The result: high-quality grey iron moulded and machined entirely in-house, giving us complete control over material quality and dimensional accuracy.
