Best Borewell Submersible Pump Brands in India:
Motor Specs, HP Ratings & Price Comparison

Updated: August 2026 11 min read S.K.B. Industries, Coimbatore

Selecting the right borewell submersible pump and water pump motor is one of the most critical decisions for farmers, residential homeowners, building contractors, and commercial facility managers in India. With groundwater levels varying from 100 feet to over 800 feet across different states, matching motor horsepower (HP), stage count, and winding material is vital for long-term operational efficiency.

In this guide, we evaluate the best borewell pump brands in India, compare 1 HP and 2 HP borewell motor pricing benchmarks, and explain why 100% copper winding remains the gold standard in water pump engineering.

1. Key Technical Criteria for Selecting Borewell Submersible Motors

Before reviewing manufacturers and prices, evaluate these core engineering parameters:

  • Total Dynamic Head (Operating Meters/Feet): The vertical lift required from the borewell depth to the overhead tank plus pipe friction loss. V4 submersibles cover up to 650m heads.
  • Borewell Outer Casing Diameter (V4 vs V6): V4 pumps (4-inch diameter) fit 4.5-inch and 6-inch borewells, whereas V6 pumps (6-inch diameter) deliver higher discharge volumes for agricultural irrigation.
  • Winding Material (100% Copper vs Aluminium): Copper winding offers 40% lower electrical resistance, preventing motor burnout during low voltage fluctuations (160V–240V).
  • Stainless Steel (SS) Casing vs Cast Iron (CI): Grade 304 SS pump bodies provide complete corrosion resistance in hard or acidic groundwater.

4. Why 100% Copper Winding Matters for Water Pump Longevity

In rural and semi-urban Indian electrical grids, voltage drops down to 160V are common. Aluminium-wound motors generate excess internal heat during low voltage operation, deteriorating insulation and causing premature coil failure.

Every SKB Water Pump is engineered with 100% high-purity electrolytic copper winding. This ensures lower operating temperature, longer bearing life, and reliable restarting under fluctuating line voltages.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average price of a 1 HP borewell submersible pump in India?

A genuine 100% copper-wound 1 HP borewell submersible pump in India ranges between ₹9,500 and ₹16,500 depending on stage count, Stainless Steel body grade, and whether sourced through traditional retail channels or direct OEM factory dispatches from SKB Pumps.

How deep can a 2 HP borewell motor pump water?

A 2 HP multi-stage borewell submersible pump can operate efficiently at total operating heads up to 350 to 500 feet (100m to 150m), making it ideal for deep agricultural borewells and multi-story residential sumps.

Why is 100% copper winding essential for borewell water pump motors?

100% copper winding provides high electrical conductivity, lower energy loss, superior heat dissipation, and wide voltage fluctuation tolerance (160V-240V), preventing motor burnout in rural and agricultural regions.
