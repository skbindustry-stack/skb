How to Choose the Best Water Pump for Home,
Agriculture & Solar Irrigation Schemes in India

Updated: August 2026 10 min read S.K.B. Industries, Coimbatore

Whether you need a reliable best water pump for home overhead tanks, a heavy-duty agriculture water pump for crop irrigation, or a motor compatible with government solar water pump schemes (PM-KUSUM), selecting the appropriate pump technology determines energy bills, discharge volume, and motor lifespan.

In this comprehensive guide, we break down domestic, agricultural, and solar water pump selection criteria, supported by SKB Pumps (S.K.B. Industries, Coimbatore) 120+ model product engineering.

1. Selecting the Best Water Pump for Home Use

Residential domestic water systems generally require pumps engineered for quick priming, quiet operation, and low power consumption:

Self-Priming Regenerative Pumps (0.5 HP – 1.0 HP)

Ideal for pumping water from ground sumps to overhead tanks in 1 to 3 story homes. Models like SKB TRON (R050SE) and CRT (R100C) deliver operating heads up to 45 meters with brass impellers.

Pressure Booster Pumps & Systems

Designed to maintain constant water pressure across modern showers, multi-bathroom apartments, and automatic line systems (ratings 0.8 HP to 2.0 HP paired with 24L to 100L pressure tanks).

Vertical Submersible & Jet Pumps

Compact SS body vertical submersibles (S0525V, S1525V) for tight residential sumps, and deep suction jet pumps (S0532J, S1532J) for suction depths up to 60 meters.

2. Choosing Agriculture Water Pumps for Farm Irrigation

Agricultural water management demands continuous heavy-duty discharge to handle large acreages of paddy, sugarcane, banana, or coconut plantations:

  • Openwell Submersible Pumps: Placed at the bottom of open farm wells or rivers. SKB SENA and Heavy-Duty series (1.0 HP to 3.0 HP) deliver massive volume flow up to 65x50 mm pipe discharge ports.
  • Borewell Compressor Pumps: Purpose-built for deep borewells containing mud, sand, or silt where standard impellers wear down. SKB Belt & Mono Compressor series handle up to 200-meter operating heads.
  • Centrifugal Monoblocks: High-discharge single and 3-phase monoblock pumps (up to 80x80 mm suction/delivery) engineered for flood irrigation and open canal transfer.

3. Solar Water Pump Schemes (PM-KUSUM) & Off-Grid Pumping

Under India's PM-KUSUM Scheme, government initiatives provide up to 60% subsidies for farmers installing solar photovoltaic water pumps. Key solar pump considerations include:

  • Wide Voltage & Frequency Operation: Solar PV arrays generate variable DC power throughout the day. Water pump motors must operate smoothly under fluctuating power outputs.
  • 100% Copper Winding Motor Efficiency: High electrical conductivity reduces heat loss, maximizing water discharge per watt of solar panel capacity.
  • Dry-Run Digital Control Panels: Paired with digital starters that automatically shut off the motor during dry water table conditions to prevent thermal damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which type of water pump is best for home overhead tank lifting?

For 1 to 3 story residential houses, a 0.5 HP or 1.0 HP self-priming regenerative pump (like SKB TRON or CRT series) with aluminium/cast-iron body and brass impeller provides fast suction and reliable tank filling.

What HP pump is required for agricultural flood irrigation?

For open agricultural wells and canals, 2.0 HP to 3.0 HP centrifugal monoblock or heavy-duty openwell submersible pumps with wide discharge pipes (50mm to 80mm) deliver maximum water flow.

How does the PM-KUSUM solar water pump scheme benefit farmers?

The PM-KUSUM scheme provides up to 60% government subsidies for farmers installing standalone solar photovoltaic water pumping systems (3 HP to 10 HP), eliminating grid electricity dependence.
