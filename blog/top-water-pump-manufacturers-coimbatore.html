1. Why Coimbatore Dominates the Indian Water Pump Industry

Coimbatore's pump manufacturing ecosystem features an integrated engineering supply chain that includes precision foundries, dynamic balancing centers, CNC machining units, and BIS-certified motor testing laboratories. Key advantages include:
