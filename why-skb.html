Why Thousands Choose
SKB Pumps

Quality isn't a claim — it's certified, tested, and backed by 20+ years of manufacturing experience from India's engineering capital.

01

ISO 9001:2008 Certified

Independently certified by Guardian/UKAS. Our quality management system meets global standards — Cert. Regn. No. 765546 confirms it.

02

5 ISI Certifications

IS 8472 · IS 9079 · IS 12225 · IS 9283 · IS 14220 — five different pump categories certified by the Bureau of Indian Standards. Not one, but five.

03

100% Copper Winding

Every motor uses 100% copper windings — not aluminium. Better conductivity, lower heat generation, and a significantly longer motor life.

04

12-Month Guarantee

All SKB pumps carry a 12-month guarantee against manufacturing defects. We stand behind every pump we make — no exceptions.

05

Wide Voltage 160–240V

Designed for Indian voltage realities. Our motors run reliably from 160V to 240V — handling the fluctuations common in rural and semi-urban grids.

06

SS 410 Grade Shaft

Stainless steel SS 410 grade shaft — dynamically precision balanced, corrosion resistant, and built to handle high axial loads without wear.

07

In-House Foundry

Bindhu Foundry — our own grey iron casting and machining unit. Complete control over material quality from raw casting to finished component.

08

Made in Coimbatore

Coimbatore is India's pump manufacturing capital. 20+ years of local engineering expertise, a strong supply chain, and an R&D team constantly innovating.
